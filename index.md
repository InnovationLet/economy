InnovationLet Economy is the economics desk of the InnovationLet network. We cover the forces that move prices, jobs and trade, and we trace them back to the companies and households they land on. No forecast theatre, no confident takes without a source. Just the mechanism, the data, and who it hits.

## What we cover

- **Policy**: central banks, fiscal decisions, and the rules that ripple through every market.
- **Trade**: tariffs, supply chains, and the logistics that quietly decide what things cost.
- **Labor**: employment, wages, and the mismatch between open roles and available skills.
- **Business cycle**: demand, credit, and the signals that show up before the headlines do.

## How we report

We follow primary sources first: statistical releases, central bank statements, and government datasets. When we cite a number, we link it. When the data is incomplete, we say that instead of guessing. The desk publishes daily, with a longer brief each week for subscribers.

Policy, trade, labor, and the business cycle. Every story is filed under one of those four areas.
